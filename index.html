📖 Learn Division

Division is sharing things equally among groups! If you have 12 🍕 slices and want to share them with 4 friends, how many does each person get?

4 friends:
12 ÷ 4 = 3

Each friend gets 3 slices! That's division! 🎉

Think of division as the opposite of multiplication!

3 × 4 = 12
12 ÷ 4 = 3
12 ÷ 3 = 4

To solve 20 ÷ 4, ask yourself: "What times 4 equals 20?"

5 × 4 = 20, so 20 ÷ 4 = 5! ⚡

Tip: Remember your multiplication tables!

Use the buttons to set up a division problem and see it visualized!
